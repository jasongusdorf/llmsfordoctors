## International

**US-Iran: a second night of strikes, and the ceasefire collapses.** The United States struck roughly 90 more Iranian military targets on Friday, on top of the 80-plus hit the night before, in what CENTCOM framed as an effort to degrade Iran's ability to menace shipping in the Strait of Hormuz, air defenses, coastal surveillance, missile and drone stores, naval and logistics sites. President Trump declared on Truth Social that "the Cease Fire is OVER!" while simultaneously saying Iran had asked to continue talks and the US had agreed. So the posture is contradictory by design: bombing and negotiating at once. Sources: [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/07/10/world/live-news/iran-war-trump), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/liveblog/2026/7/10/iran-war-live-fresh-attacks-on-iran-as-us-says-talks-still-on).

**The nuclear flashpoint at Bushehr.** Iranian officials say projectiles struck the *perimeter* of the Bushehr Nuclear Power Plant (along with a nearby military base and fishing pier); the US has denied being behind a separate wave of unexplained explosions across southern Iran. No casualties were reported at the plant itself. Iran retaliated by firing 10 ballistic missiles at Jordan's Azraq air base, with drones intercepted over Kuwait and Bahrain and sirens in the Gulf. Developing / partly single-sourced: the exact nature and attribution of the Bushehr-area strikes rests heavily on Iranian provincial statements, treat specifics as unconfirmed. Sources: [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/iran-fires-10-missiles-at-jordan-after-us-strikes-reported-near-bushehr-nuclear-plant/), [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/mena/us-launches-new-strikes-on-iran-hits-bushehr-irgc-facility-chabahar-port-and-iranshahr-airport-as-kuwait-intercepts-drone-attacks-and-air-raid-sirens-sound-in-bahrain-1.500601758).

**Ukraine: the war of refineries and civilians.** Russian aerial bombs killed at least five people, including a teenager, in Kramatorsk and Zaporizhzhia (the latter home to Europe's largest nuclear reactor). Ukraine, in turn, is squeezing Russian fuel: Kyiv says it hit two major refineries (Ilsky and Ust-Luga), an oil terminal and depot, and struck roughly 18 vessels overnight in the Sea of Azov. At the UN, the Security Council heard that Russian attacks in June killed at least 265 Ukrainian civilians and injured over 1,800, the worst monthly toll since the war's opening months. Ukraine's commander-in-chief Syrskyi says active defense has cut the pace of Russian advances by more than half in the first half of 2026. Source: [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/7/10/russian-attacks-kill-four-as-ukraine-continues-to-target-oil-infrastructure).

**Spain: deadly wildfire amid a brutal heatwave.** One of Spain's deadliest wildfires killed at least 11 people (initially reported as 12) with 23 missing, after a blaze broke out Thursday night near the Sierra de Los Filabres in Almeria. Several victims, thought to be mostly or entirely foreign tourists, were found in burned-out vehicles, apparently caught trying to flee. It comes as Western Europe endures its third heatwave in six weeks, with France near 40°C; Spain saw over 1,000 excess heat deaths in June alone. Sources: [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/07/10/nx-s1-5888160/southern-spain-wildfire-almeria), [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/07/10/europe/wildfires-southern-spain-deaths-intl).

## US

**Sanctions and superlatives on Iran.** The Treasury imposed fresh sanctions on Tehran following the Hormuz ship attacks, the latest in a string of retaliatory measures. Trump's rhetoric escalated in parallel: he claimed "1000 Missiles are Locked and Loaded and aimed at Iran" should any attempt be made on his life, and said the US military is "ready, willing, and able" to "completely decimate and destroy all areas of Iran" over a one-year window. Read alongside the "talks are on" messaging, it's brinkmanship as negotiating leverage. Source: [MSNBC live](https://www.ms.now/liveblog/liveblog-iran-us-war-news-today-july-10-2026).

**Elections and housing on the domestic ledger.** Trump fired members of a key federal election commission, the latest step in an effort to assert control over the voting process. Separately, a bipartisan housing-affordability bill is on track to become law *without* his signature. And in the not-quite-news-but-telling column, Palm Beach International Airport has officially been renamed President Donald J. Trump International Airport. Sources: [The Pavlovic Today](https://thepavlovictoday.com/trumps-day-in-office-july-10-2026/), [PBS NewsHour](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/show/july-10-2026-pbs-news-hour-full-episode).

## Israel

**A "ceasefire" that keeps moving the line.** Nine months after the US-brokered truce, NPR reports Israel's military now controls close to 70% of Gaza, up from roughly half at the ceasefire's start, having designated a new north-south "orange zone" in March that added about 10% more territory. The strip has been effectively split in two, with some two million Palestinians compressed into the western coastal half. The UN humanitarian office estimates roughly 200 Palestinians have been killed near the shifting lines since the truce began. Trump's peace plan, Israeli withdrawal, new Gaza governance, Hamas disarmament, remains stalled. Source: [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/07/10/nx-s1-5887357/israel-gaza-war-trump-ceasefire-military-control).

**Northern front: US shoring up the Hizbollah truce.** The FT reports Washington has sent a team to Beirut to shore up the Israel-Hizbollah ceasefire, a notable move to keep Lebanon from becoming a second front while the Iran confrontation runs hot. Source: [Financial Times](https://www.ft.com/world).

## Economic & Markets

**Records despite the Gulf.** Wall Street closed the week at a record: the S&P 500 rose 0.42% to 7,575.39, the Nasdaq added 0.29% to 26,281.61, and the Dow gained about 150 points to 52,637. AI names led, Nvidia +~4%, Meta +~6% after announcing a custom AI chip (targeting 14 GW of compute by 2027). Jobless claims fell to 215,000, below expectations. Notably, oil stayed relatively tame despite the Hormuz strikes, reflecting market skepticism that full-scale war returns. Sources: [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/stock-market-news-july-10-075400187.html), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/09/stock-market-today-live-updates.html).

**SK Hynix's blockbuster Nasdaq debut.** The Korean memory-chip giant opened at \$170 (ADRs priced at \$149), jumped ~14%, and raised \$26.5 billion to fund expansion, a striking vote of confidence in the AI hardware buildout. Source: [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/coverage/stock-market-today/2026/07/10/stock-market-today-july-10-markets-edge-higher-and-sk-hynix-soars-on-debut/).

**Warsh remakes the Fed.** Fed Chair Kevin Warsh named leaders of five outside task forces to rethink core Fed operations, communications, the balance sheet, economic data, productivity/jobs, and the inflation framework, with recommendations due by year-end. The roster is eye-catching: former BoE governor Mervyn King, former RBI governor Raghuram Rajan, former Brazil central banker Arminio Fraga, Harvard's Raj Chetty, ex-Walmart CEO Doug McMillon, and venture capitalist Marc Andreessen (co-leading a productivity task force centered on AI's economic impact). An unusually outsider-heavy, AI-forward attempt to reengineer the central bank. Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/09/kevin-warsh-names-members-of-his-federal-reserve-task-forces-including-marc-andreessen-doug-mcmillon.html), [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/business/2026/07/09/federal-reserve-enlists-marc-andreessen-advise-ai-under-warsh/).

**Apple v. OpenAI.** In a remarkable reversal for two 2024 partners, Apple sued OpenAI in the Northern District of California, alleging trade-secret theft "at every level." Apple claims OpenAI, via its hardware chief Tang Tan, a former Apple VP, and other ex-Apple hires, solicited confidential product designs, manufacturing processes and supply-chain strategy to build its own consumer AI hardware, including allegations that a departing employee downloaded dozens of confidential files on unreleased products. Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/10/apple-openai-lawsuit-trade-secrets.html), [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-10/apple-sues-openai-for-trade-secret-theft-in-blockbuster-case).

## Worth reading, New Yorker & long-form

**"Something Is Very Wrong with Modern Longevity Science," Dhruv Khullar.** A physician-writer's look at a provocative new book, *Morbid: Debunking Modern Longevity Science*, which argues that error and outright fraud have distorted aging research, contending that many of the world's "oldest" people aren't as old as claimed, and puncturing the hype around blue zones. Squarely in your wheelhouse: rigorous, skeptical, and about how bad data becomes conventional wisdom. [The New Yorker](https://www.newyorker.com/magazine).

**"Did a Climber Leave His Girlfriend to Die at the Top of a Mountain?"** A long, morally uneasy reconstruction of a high-altitude death, the kind of narrative feature that turns a single decision on a ridgeline into a meditation on obligation, survival, and blame. [The New Yorker](https://www.newyorker.com/magazine).
