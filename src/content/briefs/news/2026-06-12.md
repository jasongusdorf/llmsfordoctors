## International

**Trump cancels Iran strikes, declares a "settlement."** The single biggest story of the cycle: late Thursday President Trump announced he had *canceled* scheduled strikes and bombings against Iran "this evening," writing that discussions had "been brought to the highest level of Iranian leadership and approved." He told reporters, "We just made a great settlement of the war with Iran... subject to finalization of documents," floating a signing "maybe in Europe" within days. The framework reportedly extends the existing ceasefire 60 days and hinges on Iran reopening the Strait of Hormuz, with the US lifting its naval blockade in exchange. Sources: [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/06/11/nx-s1-5854970/trump-iran-peace-deal-cancel-strikes), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/6/12/stocks-markets-surge-as-trump-calls-off-strikes-on-iran-touts-peace-deal).

**Read with caution, developing and partly contradictory.** Iran has *not* publicly confirmed a deal; its foreign ministry says only that an MOU is "under consideration." Some outlets simultaneously reported continued tit-for-tat, Iran's IRGC claiming missile launches at a US-linked base in Jordan (Jordan said it intercepted them, no casualties), and US forces downing Iranian drones near Hormuz. Net read: a real de-escalation push is underway, but "war is over" is premature until documents are signed and Hormuz actually reopens.

**The longer arc.** This 2026 Iran war has been punctuated by repeated, fragile ceasefires, an initial truce back in April, mediated by Pakistan with Chinese involvement, that was extended and then "extensively violated," followed by a US naval blockade after Islamabad talks collapsed. Thursday's announcement is the most concrete settlement signal yet, but the base case should be skepticism given how many "imminent deals" have come and gone. Source: [Wikipedia background](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Iran_war_ceasefire).

**Maritime toll.** India confirmed three of its sailors were killed in an attack on a vessel off Shinas, Oman, the third Gulf-area incident linked to US action this week, a reminder of the human and shipping cost still accruing even as diplomacy accelerates. Source: [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/6/11/india-says-incident-on-vessel-off-oman-confirms-3-dead-in-seperate-strike).

**Ukraine.** The Institute for the Study of War assesses Ukraine has gained a tactical drone overmatch and is intensifying mid-range strikes as Russian recruitment sags. Overnight, a fire hit the Afipsky oil refinery in Krasnodar Krai, and Ukraine says it knocked out Crimea's Armiansk bridge, hitting ~50 Russian military vehicles. Russia launched two Iskander-M missiles and 221 drones in return. Source: [ISW via Kyiv Post](https://www.kyivpost.com/post/78006).

## US

**Boelter pleads guilty.** Vance Boelter, 58, pleaded guilty in federal court in Minneapolis to the June 2025 assassination of former Minnesota House Speaker Melissa Hortman and her husband Mark, and the shooting of state Sen. John Hoffman and his wife. Disguised as a police officer, he carried out the attacks roughly a year ago. He accepted two consecutive life sentences plus 40 years, a deal that takes the death penalty off the table; he admitted shooting Hortman point-blank in the head, drawing audible crying in the courtroom. Sources: [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/06/11/us/vance-boelter-federal-plea-minnesota-lawmaker-killing), [MPR News](https://www.mprnews.org/story/2026/06/11/vance-boelter-plea-minnesota-lawmaker-attacks-hortman-hoffman).

**Section 702 lapse.** A core foreign-surveillance authority is set to expire Saturday after the House failed, 198-218, to pass a three-week extension (it needed a two-thirds majority). The standoff is political: House Minority Leader Hakeem Jeffries says Democrats will withhold support until Trump reverses naming Bill Pulte acting DNI. Backers, including Sen. Grassley, warn of a security gap; 702 has been credited with helping thwart the 2024 Taylor Swift Vienna concert plot. Source: [Washington Times](https://www.washingtontimes.com/news/2026/jun/11/foreign-surveillance-law-set-expire-congress-leaves-town-rejecting/).

**Pentagon false alarm.** The Pentagon locked down floors two through five and partially evacuated Thursday after a sensor flagged possible anthrax. It was a malfunction; spokesman Sean Parnell said normal operations resumed by ~1:31 p.m., with follow-up testing confirming no hazard. Sources: [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/06/11/politics/pentagon-lockdown-evacuation), [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/dc-md-va/2026/06/11/pentagon-lockdown-hazardous-materials-investigation/).

**SCOTUS blocks a nitrogen execution.** In a one-line order, the Supreme Court declined Alabama's request to proceed with the nitrogen-hypoxia execution of Jeffery Lee (convicted of a 1998 double murder, scheduled July 11). A district judge had found the protocol unconstitutionally cruel under the Eighth Amendment. Justices Thomas, Alito, and Gorsuch dissented. It's a notable pause after eight prior US nitrogen executions, seven in Alabama. Sources: [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/06/11/nx-s1-5855742/supreme-court-alabama-execution), [CBS](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/jeffrey-lee-nitrogen-gas-execution-appeal/).

## Israel

**"Not a party" to the deal.** Netanyahu pushed back on Trump's framing, with a senior Israeli official telling Channel 12 that Jerusalem had not been informed of any finalized US-Iran deal, seemingly contradicting Trump's claim that all parties, Israel included, had signed off. Netanyahu said Trump assured him any agreement bars Iran from nuclear weapons and removes its enriched material; Tehran, meanwhile, publicly insists on its right to enrich. That gap, no enrichment vs. a right to enrich, is the crux that could still sink the whole thing. Source: [Times of Israel, June 12 liveblog](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog-june-12-2026/).

**On the ground.** The IDF said it killed several Hezbollah operatives trying to flee a major tunnel system near Beaufort Castle in southern Lebanon. Separately, the IDF and police foiled an attempt to smuggle dozens of firearms from Jordan into the West Bank, recovering 23 handguns and four assault rifles and arresting a suspect. Source: [Times of Israel, June 11 liveblog](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog-june-11-2026/).

**Domestic.** Tel Aviv held its Pride Parade Thursday under the slogan "Vote with your feet," while police braced for Haredi demonstrators attempting to block commuter trains as they had a week earlier. The IDF also elevated Guy Markizeno and Tal Politis to the General Staff. Source: [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog-june-12-2026/).

## Economic & Markets

**Wholesale inflation runs hot.** May's Producer Price Index jumped 1.1% on the month, nearly double the 0.6% expected, pushing the annual rate to 6.5%, the highest since November 2022. Energy did most of the damage: wholesale gasoline surged ~23% month-over-month and ~70% year-over-year, a direct echo of the Iran-driven oil shock. Core PPI rose 0.4%; pipeline pressures (processed goods +3.5%, unprocessed +4.9%) suggest more is heading toward consumer prices in the coming weeks. Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/11/producer-price-index-may-2026-.html), [CBS](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/ppi-report-today-may-2026-highest-since-2022/).

**Markets celebrate the truce.** Equities ripped on the Iran de-escalation: the Dow climbed more than 930 points, the S&P 500 rose ~1.8%, and the Nasdaq jumped ~2.5%. Asia followed hard, South Korea's Kospi surged over 8%, the Nikkei +4%. Brent crude eased ~1% to below \$89.50 on hopes Hormuz reopens. Analysts caution the rally needs an actual signed deal and a real reopening to hold. Sources: [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/6/12/stocks-markets-surge-as-trump-calls-off-strikes-on-iran-touts-peace-deal), [STL News](https://www.stl.news/u-s-stock-market-today-thursday-june-11-2026/).

**The Fed's bind.** The Fed has held rates and signaled just one cut for 2026; a 6.5% PPI print with an energy shock still feeding through makes the case for cuts harder, even as a peace deal could relieve oil prices. Annual bank stress-test results land June 24. The tension to watch: a war ending (disinflationary for energy) against inflation already baked into the pipeline. Source: [Yahoo Finance / Federal Reserve](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/live/fed-meeting-live-updates-federal-reserve-holds-rates-steady-forecasts-1-rate-cut-in-2026-180216872.html).
