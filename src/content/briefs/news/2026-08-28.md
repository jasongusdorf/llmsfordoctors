## International

**Himalayan flash flood kills 470+, with roughly 1,500 still missing**\
A glacier collapse Wednesday sent a wall of ice, water, rock and mud down the Bhote Koshi valley, destroying villages, bridges and hydropower facilities across the Nepal-Tibet border. Confirmed deaths now exceed 470, with about 1,535 missing, including roughly 644 foreign nationals from more than 30 countries; the State Department has cited about 90 Americans and Indian officials about 178 Indian nationals among them. Some 1,550 people have been rescued, 800 by helicopter, with 30,000 Nepali security personnel and 500 Chinese troops deployed. The new danger is a debris-dammed barrier lake straddling the border, holding about 2 million cubic meters of water and projected to reach 5 million within three days, which has paused rescue work near the border and triggered evacuation warnings. Roughly 42 km of road is gone, cutting ground access entirely. DEVELOPING Casualty and missing figures are moving hourly and the dead/missing split is not yet reliable.\

**King Harald V of Norway dies at 89; Crown Prince Haakon succeeds him**\
Harald V died early Friday at Oslo University Hospital after being admitted with haemolytic anaemia, a rare blood disorder. He had reigned 35 years, was Europe's oldest reigning monarch, and was the first Norwegian king born on Norwegian soil since the fourteenth century. Despite years of health problems he repeatedly refused to abdicate, citing the lifetime oath he swore to parliament. **Federal judge freezes Trump's mail-ballot restrictions, four days after SCOTUS lifted her last order**\
Judge Indira Talwani issued a temporary restraining order Thursday blocking the Postal Service from implementing Trump's mail-ballot rules for at least 14 days. In an 11-page decision she called the plan an unconstitutional attempt to usurp state authority over elections, writing that no party had identified any congressional delegation permitting USPS to regulate election mail. The Supreme Court had lifted her earlier injunction on Aug 24 on narrow procedural grounds without reaching the merits. Trump's March executive order directed USPS to build a system for blocking delivery of ballots to voters absent from state-submitted lists; the 95-page implementation plan released this month caused widespread confusion among election officials.
