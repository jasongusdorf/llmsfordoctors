**TL;DR , ** The US–Iran ceasefire has collapsed: Trump declared it “over,” US forces struck ~90 targets across two nights (including rail bridges on the route to Khamenei’s burial), and Iran fired back at Gulf bases. Iran buried its slain supreme leader in Mashhad amid a power vacuum, while Israel signals it may join the fight pending Trump’s green light. Away from the war: Trump handed Ukraine a Patriot-production win at a fractious NATO summit, and markets rose on a chip rally even as the Middle East burned.

## International

### US–Iran war reignites as ceasefire collapses

The central story this morning. Speaking at the NATO summit in Ankara, President Trump declared the June ceasefire with Iran “over” and further talks a “waste of time.” US Central Command says American forces struck roughly 90 Iranian targets over two consecutive nights, air-defense systems, coastal radar, missile and drone depots, naval and logistics sites, framed as degrading Iran’s ability to threaten navigation through the Strait of Hormuz. Strikes hit the perimeter of the Bushehr nuclear power plant (the reactor itself reportedly undamaged) and, per FT, railway bridges on the route to Mashhad. Iran’s Health Ministry reports at least 14 killed since bombing resumed. Tehran retaliated against US-allied bases in Bahrain, Kuwait and Qatar; Jordan says it intercepted eight missiles in its airspace. Pakistan and Qatar are trying to broker a return to talks. Fast-moving and partly single-sourced on casualty/target counts, figures come largely from CENTCOM and Iranian officials and should be treated as provisional. Sources: [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/world/2026/07/08/trump-declares-ceasefire-with-iran-has-ended/), [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/07/09/world/live-news/iran-war-trump), [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/world/iran/us-iran-new-attacks-trump-ceasefire-over-gulf-hormuz-rcna353615), NYT/FT (read in full).

### Khamenei buried in Mashhad; a void at the top

Iran interred its supreme leader, Ali Khamenei, killed on 28 February in a joint US–Israeli strike on his Tehran office at the war’s outset, at the Shrine of Imam Reza in Mashhad in the early hours of Friday, closing a week-long funeral that drew 15 million-plus mourners (some chanting “Kill Donald Trump”). The NYT’s lead international analysis stresses the resulting vacuum: designated successor Ayatollah Mojtaba Khamenei has not appeared in public since his appointment and skipped his father’s burial, fueling speculation about his health and who is actually running a divided regime. NYT also asks the harder question, after bombing and a failed accord, whether the administration has any “Plan C” for Iran beyond more economic warfare and airstrikes. Sources: [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/7/9/iran-prepares-to-bury-slain-leader-khamenei-after-mass-funeral-ceremonies), [Jerusalem Post](https://www.jpost.com/middle-east/iran-news/article-902045), [background](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_funeral_of_Ali_Khamenei), NYT (read in full).

### NATO summit: Trump berates allies, then hands Ukraine a win

At the Ankara summit Trump publicly lashed European allies over spending before pivoting to a notable concession: a US license for Ukraine to manufacture its own Patriot air-defense systems, something Zelensky has sought for years, alongside an allied pledge of roughly \$80 billion in defense aid for 2026. Meeting Zelensky, Trump praised his wartime performance and floated a “security package” and an eventual deal to end the war. The backdrop was grim: Russian missiles and drones hit Kyiv and other cities overnight, killing at least three. Sources: [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/07/09/nx-s1-5887053/trump-nato-zelenskyy), [France 24](https://www.france24.com/en/europe/20260708-trump-lashes-nato-allies-during-key-summit-ankara), [Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/nato-summit-trump-rages-on-allies-but-hands-ukraine-a-win/).

### ICC in crisis; Sudan case advances

An internal International Criminal Court report concluded that chief prosecutor Karim Khan abused his authority through a sexual relationship with a junior staff member and tried to discourage her from pursuing misconduct allegations; member states are set to vote on 24 July on whether to remove him. Separately, the ICC says it has reached a “breakthrough,” with concrete evidence tying leaders of Sudan’s Rapid Support Forces to war crimes and civilian massacres in Darfur (el-Fasher, el-Geneina). Source: [Just Security](https://www.justsecurity.org/146314/early-edition-july-9-2026/) and wire reporting.

## US

### New Air Force One lacks defensive countermeasures

NYT’s top story: officials say the incoming presidential aircraft lacks the defensive countermeasure systems carried by the previous model, which experts warn poses a real risk on overseas travel. The White House defended the plane’s safety. Source: [New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/) (read in full).

### Fatal ICE shooting in Houston; enforcement surge

A man, identified as Lorenzo Salgado Araujo, was killed by a federal agent in Houston during an ICE search; officials say agents wrongly believed a passenger resembled a suspect and the encounter escalated to gunfire. It lands amid a broader NYT package on surging ICE arrests, upended lives, and detention conditions (contaminated food, unwashed hands). Source: [New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/) (read in full).

### Trump moves on election machinery and the midterms

Trump dismissed members of the Election Assistance Commission, an independent body that helps states run elections, just months before November’s midterms, part of what NYT frames as an effort to “tip the scales.” In related politics, Maine Senate candidate Graham Platner suspended his campaign amid an accusation, scrambling Democrats’ plans; NYT reports on Maine women who believe the accuser yet mourn the kind of campaign they’d hoped for. Sources: [Democracy Now](https://www.democracynow.org/2026/7/9/headlines), NYT (read in full).

## Israel

### Israel weighs joining the strikes on Iran

Per Kan public broadcaster, elements in Israel want to renew strikes on Iran and have sent Washington a request to join the current US campaign, awaiting Trump’s go-ahead; Israel is wary of acting alone. IDF officials told the Jerusalem Post the military is *not* planning to be drawn into the US–Iran exchanges “at this time” but is ready for any eventuality. Trump reportedly briefed Netanyahu on “American moves in the Gulf” by phone. Single-broadcaster sourcing on the “request to join”, treat as developing. Sources: [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog_entry/reports-israel-willing-to-join-us-strikes-on-iran-waiting-for-trump-okay/), [Jerusalem Post](http://www.jpost.com/middle-east/iran-news/article-902107).

### Gaza: Israel controls ~70% of the strip

NPR reports Israel’s military now controls nearly 70% of Gaza, up from about half when the US brokered the October 2025 ceasefire, with Netanyahu describing an incremental plan to surround Hamas (“First, 70%. Let’s go for that”). The UN says around 200 Palestinians have been killed near the shifting lines since the ceasefire took effect. Trump’s peace plan, Israeli withdrawal, new Gaza governance, Hamas disarmament, has stalled; Hamas announced on 6 July it would dissolve its civil administration. Sources: [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/07/10/nx-s1-5887357/israel-gaza-war-trump-ceasefire-military-control), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/7/6/israeli-attacks-on-gaza-kill-at-least-6-as-ceasefire-violations-continue), [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/07/06/middleeast/hamas-dissolves-gaza-government-intl).

### Beersheba attack plot foiled

Four Arab Israelis were arrested and indicted over an alleged plan to carry out a mass shooting at Beersheba’s central station and attacks on security forces. Source: [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/).

## Economic & Markets

### Warsh’s Fed reform takes shape

New Fed chair Kevin Warsh named the leaders of five outside task forces charged with overhauling the central bank, covering communications, the balance sheet, data quality, and productivity/jobs/AI, plus a review of the inflation framework. Notable names: Marc Andreessen and Charles Jones (productivity, jobs & AI); former BoE governor Mervyn King, Peter Fisher and Arminio Fraga (communications); Raj Chetty, Doug McMillon and Kevin Murphy (data); Karen Dynan, Raghuram Rajan and Jeremy Stein (balance sheet). Recommendations are due by year-end, an unusually outsider-heavy, ideologically pointed reshaping of Fed operations. Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/09/kevin-warsh-names-members-of-his-federal-reserve-task-forces-including-marc-andreessen-doug-mcmillon.html), [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/07/09/fed-warsh-task-force-andreessen), FT (read in full).

### Stocks shrug off Iran; chips lead

Markets rose Thursday even as the US and Iran traded strikes. The Nasdaq gained 1.3% to 26,206.89, the S&P 500 rose 0.81% to 7,543.64, and the Dow added 0.27% to 52,487.41. The rally was led by semiconductors after Micron announced a plan to invest more than \$250 billion in US memory-chip manufacturing through 2035 to meet AI demand. Brent crude fell more than 2%, easing inflation fears; weekly jobless claims came in below expectations while housing data stayed soft. PepsiCo slipped ~1.8% on mixed Q2 results. Sources: [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-thursday-july-9-us-strikes-iran-223149196.html), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/08/stock-market-today-live-updates.html), [TheStreet](https://www.thestreet.com/stock-market-today/stock-market-today-dow-jones-sp-500-nasdaq-updates-july-9-2026).

### “The Tick That Hunts Down Its Hosts, Including Us”, Burkhard Bilger

A reporter-at-large piece squarely in your lane, Jason: lone-star ticks don’t just wait to be brushed against, they actively pursue people, and the alpha-gal syndrome they spread (a delayed allergy to mammalian red meat) is reshaping how people in affected regions can eat and live. A rigorous natural-history-meets-clinical read from the current “Body Issue.” ([The New Yorker](https://www.newyorker.com/magazine), July 6 & 13, 2026)

### “Are Humanoid Robots Ready to Be Deployed?”, Stephen Witt

Neo and roughly a dozen other human-form robots are scheduled to hit the consumer market, and the experts closest to them are nervous. A clear-eyed look at whether the hardware, and our expectations, are actually ready. Pairs well with David Remnick’s Comment on Haberman & Swan’s “Regime Change,” the issue’s most newsy political read. ([The New Yorker](https://www.newyorker.com/magazine), July 6 & 13, 2026)
