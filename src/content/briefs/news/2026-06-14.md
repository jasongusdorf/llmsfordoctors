**TL;DR.** The story dominating the weekend is the US–Iran war: Trump says a peace deal reopening the Strait of Hormuz will be signed today, though Tehran is publicly cooling expectations and the terms remain unconfirmed. Markets are already pricing relief, oil has fallen back toward \$85 from spring's \$126 peak. Meanwhile a US judge forced Trump's name off the Kennedy Center, South Korea jailed ex-president Yoon for 30 years, and the World Cup opened across North America.

## International

**US–Iran: a deal, maybe today, maybe not.** The biggest story by far is the apparent winding-down of the US–Iran war that began February 28 with the joint US–Israeli strikes of *Operation Epic Fury* (in which Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei was killed) and escalated into Iran's closure of the Strait of Hormuz and dueling naval blockades. President Trump posted that the agreement is "scheduled to get signed tomorrow, and immediately after it is signed, the Hormuz Strait is OPEN TO ALL." Pakistan, the lead mediator, said a deal was closer "than ever before." Tehran is notably more guarded: Foreign Ministry spokesman Esmaeil Baqaei and senior officials told Reuters signing could slip to "the coming days," urging a "wait and see" posture. Developing / partly single-sourced: per Iranian officials and Iran's Mehr news agency, the draft is a 14-point memorandum of understanding under which Iran reopens Hormuz to commercial traffic (within 30 days per Mehr), the US lifts its naval blockade and waives oil sanctions for a set period, no new US sanctions land before a final deal, roughly \$25bn in frozen Iranian assets is released, and a final agreement is negotiated over ~60 days. These terms come largely from the Iranian side and have not been confirmed by Washington in detail. A Trump official put the odds of signing "in the coming days" at ~80%. Sources: [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/world/iran/us-iran-deal-expected-reopen-strait-hormuz-signed-days-both-sides-say-rcna349916), [Al Jazeera live](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/liveblog/2026/6/14/iran-war-live-trump-says-deal-to-be-signed-today-as-tehran-urges-caution), Reuters, Mehr (June 13–14).

**South Korea jails Yoon Suk Yeol for 30 years.** The Seoul Central District Court on Friday sentenced the ousted former president to 30 years for ordering military drone flights over Pyongyang in 2024, a scheme prosecutors said was meant to manufacture a North Korean threat and justify his short-lived December 2024 martial-law declaration. Ex-defense minister Kim Yong-hyun also got 30 years; the former counterintelligence chief got 15. Yoon's team appealed immediately. The ruling stacks on an earlier life sentence in the martial-law case itself. Sources: [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/06/11/asia/south-korean-yoon-suk-yeol-drone-case-jail-intl-hnk), [UPI](https://www.upi.com/Top_News/World-News/2026/06/12/korea-Yoon-Suk-Yeol-sentenced-30-years-drone-incursion-North-Korea-martial-law/1091781254566/), NPR (June 12).

**Ukraine: EU accession talks set to open Monday.** The EU confirmed it will formally launch accession negotiations with Ukraine and Moldova on June 15, a milestone Zelensky welcomed. It lands against continued grinding combat: Russia fired ~118 long-range/Shahed-type drones overnight, Dnipropetrovsk took roughly 40 attacks on June 13, and a Ukrainian drone strike set a marine terminal ablaze in Russia's Krasnodar Krai. Sources: [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/6/8/ukraine-russia-trade-fire-as-zelenskyy-allies-back-call-for-direct-talks), [Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/) (June 13).

## US

**Trump's name comes off the Kennedy Center.** Workers began stripping the president's name from the facade of the John F. Kennedy Center for the Performing Arts early Saturday, after US District Judge Christopher Cooper ruled the rebranding, pushed through six months ago by a Trump-handpicked board, was illegal. A federal appeals court denied the DOJ's bid for an administrative stay Friday night, and the administration's request for a 12-hour extension failed; DOJ certified removal from "all physical signage on the Kennedy Center building and grounds" about an hour before the noon Saturday deadline. Sources: [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/style/2026/06/12/kennedy-center-removes-trumps-name-building/), [ABC News](https://abcnews.com/Politics/trumps-removed-kennedy-center-noon-saturday-judge/story?id=133841904), NPR (June 12–13).

Domestically, the US–Iran deal is also the day's dominant Washington story (see International), a signing would be the biggest foreign-policy event of Trump's second term and would immediately ease the energy shock that has shadowed the US economy since spring.

## Israel

**Cross-border fire continues even as the wider war cools.** Three Hezbollah drones exploded in northern Israel on Sunday morning; the IDF responded with renewed airstrikes on Beirut's southern suburbs, despite the standing Israel–Hezbollah ceasefire. The flare-ups are complicating the US–Iran endgame. Politically, opposition leader Yair Lapid panned the emerging US–Iran agreement, arguing it "fails to achieve any of Jerusalem's war goals." A contested bill to set up a political October 7 inquiry is set for a vote this week and has drawn anti-government protests. On the diplomatic side, Israel is slated to open full relations with Somaliland, with an embassy inauguration expected Monday evening. Source: [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog-june-14-2026/) live blog (June 14).

## Economic & Markets

**Oil retreats as the war premium deflates.** Crude fell Friday on deal optimism: Brent settled down 3.4% near \$87.33 and WTI off 3.2% near \$84.88, a dramatic climbdown from the spring peak above \$126/bbl when Hormuz was shut (the largest energy supply disruption since the 1970s, with ~20% of global oil and LNG normally transiting the strait). A confirmed reopening would accelerate the decline. Source: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/12/oil-prices-wti-brent-on-hopes-of-us-iran-deal-despite-tehran-pushback.html) (June 12).

**Equities near records; the Fed is the wildcard.** US stocks tracked higher on the week, S&P 500 around 7,431, Dow ~51,202, Nasdaq ~25,889, helped by hopes that a deal lifts oil sanctions and reopens Hormuz. But resilient growth plus elevated energy costs have flipped the rate narrative: CME FedWatch showed roughly 70% odds of at least one 0.25% Fed *hike* by December. Market breadth remains a concern, about 10 AI-linked names make up nearly 40% of the S&P 500's value and a near-majority of 2026 expected earnings growth. International equities (EAFE) have recovered most of their conflict-era drop; EM is up 20%+ YTD. Sources: market wraps via Schwab / U.S. Bank; CME FedWatch (week of June 7–13).

## Also notable

**The World Cup is on.** The 2026 FIFA World Cup, co-hosted by the US, Mexico and Canada, kicked off June 11 at Estadio Azteca with a ceremony featuring Shakira, Burna Boy and a roster of Latin stars. Mexico beat South Africa 2–0, its first-ever win on the tournament's opening day; openings followed in Toronto and Los Angeles on June 12. Sources: [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/06/11/sport/live-news/world-cup-mexico-south-africa), [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/world-cup-opening-ceremony-mexico-shakira/).

**Pope Leo XIV grounded in Spain.** The pope's Iberia charter back to Rome, after a weeklong visit to Spain, was held up by a technical fault Friday, prompting Spain's king to offer his private jet. A minor item, but a notable papal-travel hiccup. Source: wire reports (June 13).
