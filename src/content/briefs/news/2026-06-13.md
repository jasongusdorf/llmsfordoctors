## International

### US and Iran say a nuclear deal is close, but the guns aren’t fully silent

The headline story of the week remains the on-again push to lock in a US–Iran nuclear agreement that would trade sanctions relief for dismantling Iran’s program. A US official told reporters the framework is roughly “85% done,” contingent on Iran following through, and President Trump told Axios he expects a deal “within days,” with a signing ceremony floated for Geneva and Vice President Vance potentially attending. The Trump team says it is “confident” Israel will get on board. Developing / partly single-sourced, the “85%” figure comes from one official and terms are unconfirmed. The diplomacy sits atop a shaky ceasefire: Iran has suspended operations against Israel but warned it will resume if Israeli strikes in southern Lebanon continue. Sources: [Arab News](https://www.arabnews.com/node/2646308/middle-east), [CNN live](https://www.cnn.com/2026/06/12/world/live-news/iran-war-trump-israel).

### South Korea’s Yoon gets 30 years over the Pyongyang drone plot

A Seoul court sentenced former President Yoon Suk Yeol to 30 years in prison, finding he conspired in an October 2024 operation that sent military drones over Pyongyang to manufacture a pretext for his failed December 2024 martial-law declaration. The court convicted him of abuse of power and aiding the enemy; former Defense Minister Kim Yong Hyun received the same term. Yoon, 65 and already in custody since his impeachment was upheld, denies ordering the flights and can appeal. It is a remarkable capstone to the gravest constitutional crisis South Korea has faced in a generation. Sources: [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/06/11/asia/south-korean-yoon-suk-yeol-drone-case-jail-intl-hnk), [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/06/12/nx-s1-5856122/ousted-south-korean-president-yoon-prison-drone-flights-pyongyang), [CBC](https://www.cbc.ca/news/world/south-korea-yoon-kim-sentences-9.7233128).

### Belfast burns: anti-immigrant violence enters a fourth night

Days of rioting have swept Belfast and spread to parts of Scotland and England after a knife attack that left a local man, Stephen Ogilvie, in a coma. Rioters went door-to-door trying to identify immigrant-occupied homes; the displaced include Ugandan carers, a Ukrainian family and a Romani family, and the PSNI chief constable said a two-month-old baby had to be rescued. The fire brigade attended dozens of incidents in a single night. The Times called it a “modern-day pogrom”; Elon Musk has drawn criticism for posts amplifying the unrest. Sources: [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/world/2026/06/10/belfast-riots-mayhem-follow-alleged-stabbing-by-sudanese-asylum-seeker/), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/6/12/riots-violence-hate-anti-immigrant-unrest-spells-danger-in-belfast), [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/world/united-kingdom/belfast-riots-elon-musk-anti-immigrant-violence-stabbing-rcna349384).

The expanded 2026 FIFA World Cup, the first hosted across three nations (US, Canada, Mexico), kicked off, with the opener staged in Mexico. More on the US side below. Source: [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/06/12/nx-s1-5850736/2026-world-cup-usmnt-paraguay).

## US

### SpaceX’s blockbuster IPO mints the first trillionaire

SpaceX (Nasdaq: SPCX) staged the largest IPO in Wall Street history, selling about 555 million shares at \$135 to raise roughly \$75 billion at an implied valuation near \$1.77 trillion. Shares opened at \$150, briefly spiked more than 30% (a fleeting \$2.25T market cap), and closed up about 19% at \$160.95. The debut is widely reported to have made Elon Musk the world’s first trillionaire on paper. Beyond the spectacle, it hands SpaceX an enormous war chest for Starship and Starlink and reorders the calculus of the entire commercial-space sector. Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/12/spacex-ipo-spcx-live-updates.html), [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/business/markets/spacex-ipo-stock-price-rcna349760).

### USMNT opens its home World Cup with a statement

The US men dazzled in a 4–1 win over Paraguay to open the tournament, Folarin Balogun scored twice, Christian Pulisic tormented the Paraguayan back line, and Gio Reyna added a late goal (with an own goal also in the mix). A confident, much-needed start for the hosts. Sources: [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/soccer/match/_/gameId/760417/paraguay-united-states), [CBS Sports](https://www.cbssports.com/soccer/news/usa-paraguay-live-updates-world-cup-2026-score-result/live/).

### The surveillance number worth noting

Officials disclosed that more than 60% of the president’s daily intelligence brief now draws on information collected under FISA Section 702, a striking figure landing amid the recurring fight over reauthorizing the warrantless-surveillance tool. Expect it to feature in the next round of the privacy-vs-national-security debate. Single prominent source so far; watch for corroboration. Source: [NPR](https://www.npr.org/sections/national/).

## Israel

### Strikes in Lebanon as the ceasefire frays

The IDF carried out airstrikes on Hezbollah sites in and around Nabatieh in southern Lebanon after issuing evacuation warnings, part of the ongoing 2026 Israel–Hezbollah confrontation that has repeatedly strained the broader Israel–Iran truce. Iran has explicitly warned that continued Israeli operations in south Lebanon could trigger a resumption of its own attacks. Source: [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/latest/).

### US downs Iranian drones near the Strait of Hormuz

The US military reported shooting down two Iranian drones said to be attempting to strike ships near the Strait of Hormuz, a reminder of how close the maritime chokepoint remains to flashpoint status even as diplomats talk peace. Developing; drawn from a live wire feed, details may shift. Source: [ABC News](https://abcnews.com/International/live-updates/iran-live-updates-israel-iran-trade-strikes-trump/?id=133674243).

### Katz: no withdrawal, no halt

Defense Minister Israel Katz said Israel will continue independent operations against Iran and will not withdraw from security zones in Lebanon, Syria or Gaza, and will keep operating in the northern West Bank. The posture signals Israel intends to keep a free hand militarily even if a US–Iran deal is signed, a potential point of friction with Washington’s push to get Israel “on board.” Source: [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/latest/).

## Economic & Markets

### Best day since April

US stocks rallied Friday to their best single-session showing since April 8. The S&P 500 rose 0.5% to 7,431.46, the Dow gained 0.7% to 51,202.26, and the Nasdaq added 0.31% to 25,888.84. The drivers: SpaceX’s blockbuster open lifting sentiment, and optimism that a US–Iran deal could remove a major geopolitical tail risk. Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/11/stock-market-today-live-updates.html), [Globe and Mail](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/investing/markets/stocks/AMD/pressreleases/2436808/stock-market-news-for-jun-12-2026/).

### A hot inflation surprise lurking underneath

Cutting against the cheer: the May Producer Price Index reportedly rose 1.1% on the month, well above the ~0.6% expected and, if confirmed, the hottest monthly wholesale-inflation reading since November 2022. That is a notable upside surprise that could complicate the disinflation narrative and the rate-cut outlook; worth watching whether it holds up and how the next CPI corroborates it. Flagging the magnitude, an unusually large monthly print; confirm against the official BLS release. Meanwhile crude oil fell about 2% toward \$85 on Iran de-escalation hopes, and the University of Michigan consumer-sentiment gauge ticked up ~9% as gasoline prices eased. Source: [Schwab market update](https://www.schwab.com/learn/story/stock-market-update-open).

### Narrow leadership

One structural caution amid the rally: market breadth has thinned sharply. Only about one in three stocks has outperformed the index over the past three months, reportedly the lowest share in more than 30 years, leaving gains concentrated in a handful of mega-cap and tech names. Source: [U.S. Bank](https://www.usbank.com/investing/financial-perspectives/market-news/is-a-market-correction-coming.html).
