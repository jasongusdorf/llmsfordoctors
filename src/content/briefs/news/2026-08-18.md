Top of the Morning

The 60-day US–Iran memorandum expired overnight with nothing to replace it, and the market is now pricing the consequences directly: Brent above \$90, and the 30-year Treasury at 5.33%, its highest since 2007, in a synchronized global long-bond sell-off. Trump responded to the deadlock by threatening to bomb Oman, the ally mediating the talks. Meanwhile Kushner emerged from hours with Netanyahu with working groups but no Israeli sign-off, and the WHO confirmed the DR Congo Ebola outbreak is now the deadliest in that country's history at 2,325 dead.

## International

### Ukraine sends nearly 800 drones at the Moscow region; Russian missiles kill 10 in Kharkiv

Ukraine launched what TASS called the largest attack on the capital region in roughly two years overnight Monday into Tuesday. Russia's Defense Ministry said air defenses downed 791 drones across 15 regions plus Crimea and the Azov and Black Seas, with Moscow Mayor Sergei Sobyanin reporting about 180 intercepted over Moscow Oblast. A Wildberries e-commerce warehouse was struck and at least three people were injured, including a 10-year-old girl. Separately, a Russian missile strike on the village of Pechenihy in Kharkiv Oblast killed at least 10 and wounded eight, demolishing homes. AP describes it as the second-largest Ukrainian drone attack since January 2025, following a similar salvo two days earlier. *Note: intercept and casualty figures are official claims from each side and are not independently verified.* [AP / Al Jazeera, Aug 18](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/18/russia-says-more-than-600-drones-targeted-moscow-region)

### The US–Iran deadline lapses, and Trump threatens the mediator

The 60-day memorandum of understanding signed in June by Trump and President Masoud Pezeshkian expired Monday with no agreement and no extension. The MoU had collapsed earlier after Iran targeted ships transiting a channel along Oman's coast, triggering tit-for-tat strikes. Iran and Oman have been negotiating separately for weeks on a provisional arrangement for managing Hormuz shipping; the FT reports that push stalled more than a week ago as Tehran sought changes giving itself more control over the strait. Iran's lead negotiator Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf said Tuesday the strait stays shut until Washington lifts its naval blockade, lifts oil sanctions, releases frozen assets and halts military operations. Asked by Fox's Trey Yingst about the Oman track, Trump said: "If Oman gets in the way, we'll bomb the s--- out of them", the second such threat against Muscat since May. Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth said the Navy can blockade Iranian ports indefinitely. About a fifth of the world's oil and gas transited Hormuz before the war. [FT, Aug 17](https://www.ft.com/content/d4d07198-498d-48d1-9e54-85f1febcd070)

### DR Congo's Ebola outbreak becomes the deadliest in the country's history

The WHO said Monday the Bundibugyo-strain outbreak in eastern DRC has killed 2,325 people, passing the 2,299 deaths of the 2018–20 epidemic. There are roughly 4,945 confirmed cases and a 46% case-fatality rate. National authorities declared the outbreak on May 15; Ituri is the epicenter, with spread across six provinces and about 20 cases reported in Uganda. Globally it is now exceeded only by West Africa's 2014–16 outbreak, but it is killing considerably faster. [WHO / UN News / Al Jazeera, Aug 17](https://news.un.org/en/story/2026/08/1168154)

### Trump scales back South Korea drills, rattling Seoul

The FT reports Trump has ordered the Pentagon to cut back joint military exercises with South Korea, a step that strikes at the operational core of the alliance and has stirred broader doubts about US resolve in Asia. The NYT frames the move alongside a wider pivot: facing a quagmire in Iran, Trump is turning back toward Kim Jong-un, a familiar counterpart from a first-term diplomatic effort that ultimately failed to disarm North Korea. [FT / NYT, Aug 17–18](https://www.ft.com/world/asia-pacific)

### Indonesia quake toll climbs past 68 on Flores

The magnitude 7.7 quake that struck off Flores early Saturday, epicenter about 68 km north-northwest of Ende in East Nusa Tenggara, has killed at least 68 people as of Monday, up from initial counts in the 40s. Nearly 1,000 aftershocks have followed, the strongest at M6.1, and the quake generated localized tsunami waves. More than half a million people experienced very strong shaking. Rescuers are still digging through rubble and landslides, and evacuees report severe food shortages. *Developing, the toll is rising and figures vary by outlet.* [Al Jazeera / NPR / TIME, Aug 15–17](https://time.com/article/2026/08/17/indonesia-earthquake-damage-death-toll/)

### Zambia's Hichilema wins a second term

The Electoral Commission of Zambia declared President Hakainde Hichilema the winner in the early hours of Tuesday with 2,965,326 votes, about 61.5%, against Brian Mundubile's 1,856,217. International observers had said Saturday the August 13 vote was marred by reports of intimidation and violence. Also in the region: Morocco arrested at least 111 people over the weekend attempting to reach Spain's enclave of Ceuta, following the early-August surge in which an estimated 60,000 to 72,000 people crossed in roughly a week with at least 72 deaths. Myanmar's Min Aung Hlaing flew to Moscow Monday seeking military support and oil. [Al Jazeera / Bloomberg, Aug 18](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/18/zambias-hichilema-wins-second-term-as-president)

## United States

### Judge blocks the administration from scrapping the FBI headquarters move

US District Judge Theodore Chuang ruled Monday for Maryland and Prince George's County, finding the administration unlawfully abandoned the congressionally directed plan to build a new FBI headquarters in Greenbelt and diverted appropriated funds to the Ronald Reagan Building in downtown Washington. Chuang held that the Reagan Building selection violated 2022 and 2023 statutes requiring GSA to choose from three short-listed suburban sites, and barred FBI Director Kash Patel and Attorney General Todd Blanche from executing the move. Greenbelt was originally selected in 2023. An appeal is expected but not yet confirmed. [Washington Post / Reuters / AP, Aug 17](https://www.washingtonpost.com/national-security/2026/08/17/trump-administration-cant-scrap-plan-move-fbi-headquarters-md-judge-rules/)

### Kindergarten vaccine exemptions hit a record 4.2%

CDC data released Monday shows exemptions among US kindergartners rose to 4.2% in the 2025–26 school year from 3.6%, roughly 155,000 children, the fourth consecutive record year. Coverage slipped for every childhood vaccine tracked nationally, and about 280,000 kindergartners lacked documentation of a completed MMR series. The US has logged 2,566 confirmed measles cases so far in 2026, the highest in more than 35 years. This lands against the backdrop of an executive order signed earlier in August directing a narrowed childhood schedule and recommending MMR be split across three separate visits. *The EO detail comes from secondary summary and is worth verifying against the text.* [CNBC / The Hill / AP, Aug 17](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/17/us-kindergarten-vaccine-falls-cdc.html)

### Hawaii cleans up after Storm Lala

Lala made its closest approach to the Big Island late Saturday as a Category 1 hurricane, then weakened as it moved west Monday. Governor Josh Green said at a Monday news conference that the body of a 90-year-old woman who died in the flooding was recovered. Roughly 100 homes were swept off their foundations, tens of thousands lost power, and Hilo International Airport recorded nearly 10 inches of rain, with road closures in Hilo, Mountain View and Hawaiian Paradise. Meteorologists have called it one of the most impactful storms for the Big Island in more than 150 years. *Developing, damage assessments still updating.* [CNN / CBS / AP, Aug 16–17](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/tropical-storm-lala-big-island-hawaii-hurricane-track/)

## Israel

### Strikes on an Idlib airbase; the Lebanon front stays hot

Eight strikes hit the runway and storage facilities at the Abu al-Duhur military airbase in Idlib province early Tuesday, roughly 70 km from the Turkish border. ### The story of the morning is the long end

Long-term borrowing costs across the major economies hit multi-decade highs Tuesday. The 30-year US Treasury yield rose as high as 5.33%, its highest since 2007, having been below 5% at the start of last month. The 30-year Bund jumped to 3.78%, its highest since the eurozone crisis in 2011; 30-year French yields hit 4.9%, the highest since 2008; the 30-year gilt reached 5.86%, within touching distance of a post-1998 high set in the early weeks of the Iran war; and Japanese 30-year yields rose to 4.14%, close to their highest ever. "Long-end yields have largely been following oil prices," Jefferies' Mohit Kumar told the FT. "As oil goes to \$90 and above, inflation concerns start to dominate." Barclays' Anshul Pradhan identifies three factors: the budget deficit outlook, AI-related corporate issuance, and a changing Treasury buyer base, Big Tech is increasingly issuing long-dated debt abroad to fund AI capex, with Barclays expecting record investment-grade issuance of \$1.9tn in 2026. MUFG's Derek Halpenny is blunter: "There remains zero appetite in the US for addressing the US fiscal position and that is increasingly weighing on the long end of the curve." US debt is approaching \$40tn, and Treasury paid its highest 30-year interest cost since 2011 at last week's auction. RBC's Peter Schaffrik calls the rise "a multiyear story, and it's not over." [FT, Aug 18](https://www.ft.com/content/61354e68-c4ba-4716-b849-b332608c8c65)

Trump declared the strait "open and the US is in control" and floated declaring it US territory. A senior Iranian official told Reuters Tehran is prepared to shift to a fully offensive posture. *Fast-moving.* [Bloomberg / Reuters / CNBC, Aug 17–18](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-17/latest-oil-market-news-and-analysis-for-aug-18)

[CNBC / Reuters / PRNewswire, Aug 17–18](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/17/japan-gdp-misses-estimates-trade-exports-yen-nikkei.html)
