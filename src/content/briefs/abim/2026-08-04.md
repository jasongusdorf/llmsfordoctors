1. **Cardiology**. Primary-prevention ICD: LVEF ≤35%, NYHA II–III, on GDMT ≥3 months, and ≥40 days post-MI or ≥90 days post-revascularization. The waiting periods are the tested part.
2. **Pulmonary**. Intermediate-risk (submassive) PE with RV strain and positive troponin but normal BP: anticoagulate and monitor, do not lyse. PEITHO cut decompensation but caused major bleeding and intracranial hemorrhage with no mortality benefit.
3. **Nephrology**. RTA: type 1 can't acidify (urine pH >5.5), hypokalemia, nephrocalcinosis/stones. Type 2 proximal, pH falls <5.5 once serum bicarb drops below the reabsorptive threshold, plus Fanconi features. Type 4 is the hyperkalemic one with an acid urine.
4. **Endocrinology**. Amiodarone thyrotoxicosis: type 1 = iodine-load on nodular goiter, increased Doppler vascularity → thionamide. Type 2 = destructive thyroiditis, absent vascularity → glucocorticoids. Amiodarone often continued in type 2.
5. **Oncology**. Febrile neutropenia = ANC <500 (or expected to fall below) with a single temp ≥38.3°C or ≥38.0°C for 1 hour. MASCC ≥21 = low risk, eligible for oral cipro + amox-clav outpatient.
6. **Neurology**. Status epilepticus is most often failed by underdosed benzos: lorazepam 0.1 mg/kg IV. Second-line ESETT showed fosphenytoin 20 PE/kg, levetiracetam 60 mg/kg, and valproate 40 mg/kg all equivalent (~half seizure-free), pick by contraindication, not efficacy.
7. **Infectious disease**. S. aureus bacteremia is never "contaminant, treat 5 days": echo every patient (TEE if prosthetic material, persistent bacteremia, or no source), repeat cultures to document clearance, remove the line, ≥2 weeks IV from the first negative culture. Adjunctive rifampin did not help in ARREST.
8. **General IM / Geriatrics**. Treat osteoporosis if T-score ≤−2.5, OR any hip/vertebral fragility fracture regardless of T-score, OR osteopenia with FRAX 10-year hip ≥3% / major osteoporotic ≥20%.
9. **Gastroenterology**. Clarithromycin triple therapy is out where resistance exceeds ~15% (most of the US), bismuth quadruple 14 days first-line. Test of cure for everyone: PPI held 2 weeks, antibiotics 4 weeks, then breath or stool antigen.
10. **Rheumatology**. Antiphospholipid syndrome with thrombosis gets warfarin, not a DOAC. TRAPS stopped early for excess arterial events on rivaroxaban in triple-positive patients. Confirmatory antibody testing must be ≥12 weeks apart.

Subspecialties covered: Cardiology, Pulmonary, Nephrology, Endocrinology, Oncology, Neurology, ID, General IM/Geriatrics, GI, Rheumatology.
