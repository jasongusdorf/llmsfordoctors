1. **Cardiology**. Cardiac amyloidosis: suspect when LV wall thickening is *discordant* with low/normal QRS voltage. Before calling it ATTR on a 99mTc-PYP scan (grade 2–3 uptake), you must exclude AL with serum/urine immunofixation **plus** serum free light chains, PYP uptake cannot distinguish AL. Tafamidis (ATTR-ACT) for ATTR-CM. Avoid digoxin and nondihydropyridine CCBs.
2. **Pulmonary**. Pulmonary hypertension is a right heart cath diagnosis, not an echo diagnosis. Current hemodynamic definition: mPAP >20 mmHg; precapillary requires PCWP ≤15 with PVR >2 Wood units. The board trap is giving PAH-specific vasodilators for group 2 (left heart) or group 3 (lung disease) PH, they worsen outcomes.
3. **Gastroenterology**. Microscopic colitis: chronic watery non-bloody diarrhea in an older woman with a **normal-appearing colonoscopy**, the diagnosis is made only on random biopsies. Culprit drugs: NSAIDs, PPIs, SSRIs. Budesonide is first-line.
4. **Infectious disease**. Erythema migrans is a **clinical** diagnosis, do not order Lyme serology, early disease is often seronegative. Doxycycline for EM and for isolated facial palsy; but high-degree AV block from Lyme carditis gets IV ceftriaxone and telemetry, not oral therapy.
5. **Rheumatology**. ANCA vasculitis: PR3/c-ANCA tracks with GPA, MPO/p-ANCA with MPA. EGPA is the outlier, asthma plus eosinophilia, and ANCA is positive in only about 40%. Induction is glucocorticoids plus rituximab or cyclophosphamide.
6. **Endocrinology**. Subclinical hypothyroidism: treat if TSH >10 mIU/L, or 4.5–10 with symptoms, positive TPO antibodies, or pregnancy/pregnancy intent. In adults over 65 with mild elevation, levothyroxine did **not** improve symptoms or fatigue (TRUST), reflexive treatment is the wrong answer.
7. **Nephrology**. Contrast-associated AKI is far less common than classically taught, and the prophylaxis questions have a settled answer: N-acetylcysteine and sodium bicarbonate are **no better than isotonic saline** (PREVENT/PRESERVE). Never withhold an indicated contrast CT for suspected PE over this.
8. **Hematology**. ITP is a diagnosis of exclusion (check HIV, HCV, H. pylori; review the peripheral smear for clumping and schistocytes). Treat for bleeding or platelets <30,000/µL. Glucocorticoids first-line; add IVIG when you need a rise in 24–48 hours. Second-line: TPO receptor agonists, rituximab, or splenectomy.
9. **Allergy/Immunology**. Angioedema **without urticaria** that does not respond to epinephrine, antihistamines, or steroids is bradykinin-mediated: hereditary angioedema or ACE-inhibitor angioedema. Screen HAE with a **C4 level** (low even between attacks), then C1-inhibitor level and function. Acute HAE treatment is C1-INH concentrate, icatibant, or ecallantide.
10. **General IM / Prevention**. Aspirin for primary prevention has been walked back. ASPREE showed no cardiovascular benefit and more major bleeding (and more all-cause mortality) in healthy adults ≥70. USPSTF 2022: do **not** initiate aspirin at age ≥60; ages 40–59 with ≥10% 10-year risk is an individualized decision. Secondary prevention is unchanged.
