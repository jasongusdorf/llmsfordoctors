1. **Cardiology**. Pre-excited AF (WPW with irregular wide-complex tachycardia): AV nodal blockers, adenosine, diltiazem, verapamil, beta-blockers, digoxin, can accelerate accessory-pathway conduction and precipitate VF. Use procainamide or ibutilide, or DC cardioversion if unstable.
2. **Pulmonary**. Light's criteria call an effusion exudative if pleural/serum protein >0.5, pleural/serum LDH >0.6, or pleural LDH >2/3 the upper limit of normal serum LDH. The classic trap: a diuresed HF effusion misclassifies as exudate, a serum-to-pleural albumin gradient >1.2 g/dL says transudate.
3. **Gastroenterology**. SBP is ascitic PMN ≥250/mm³ regardless of culture. Cefotaxime plus IV albumin 1.5 g/kg day 1 and 1 g/kg day 3, albumin cuts mortality specifically when Cr >1 mg/dL, BUN >30, or bilirubin >4.
4. **Infectious disease**. Asymptomatic bacteriuria is treated in exactly two settings: pregnancy, and before a urologic procedure expected to breach the mucosa. Not for pyuria, not for a foul-smelling bag, not for a catheter, not for delirium without other findings.
5. **Rheumatology**. Suspected giant cell arteritis: start high-dose glucocorticoids immediately, before biopsy, the temporal artery biopsy stays positive for ~2 weeks on steroids. Vision loss or amaurosis → IV methylprednisolone. Tocilizumab is the steroid-sparing add-on.
6. **Endocrinology**. Screen for primary aldosteronism with a plasma aldosterone/renin ratio in resistant hypertension, hypertension with hypokalemia (spontaneous or diuretic-induced), adrenal incidentaloma, or HTN with OSA. MRAs must be off ~4–6 weeks first; ACEi/ARBs and beta-blockers shift the ratio but rarely enough to obscure a positive.
7. **Hematology**. HIT: platelet fall >50% from baseline, typically day 5–10, thrombosis not bleeding. Stop all heparin and start a non-heparin anticoagulant (argatroban, bivalirudin, fondaparinux, or a DOAC). Do not start warfarin until platelets recover, warfarin alone causes venous limb gangrene, and avoid prophylactic platelet transfusion.
8. **Oncology**. Hypercalcemia of malignancy: isotonic saline first, add zoledronic acid (works in 2–4 days), and use calcitonin only as a bridge for the first ~48 hours before tachyphylaxis. Denosumab is the answer when renal function precludes a bisphosphonate or the bisphosphonate failed. Loop diuretics only if volume overloaded.
9. **Nephrology**. Correct chronic hyponatremia no faster than ~8 mEq/L in 24 hours (slower with alcohol use, malnutrition, hypokalemia, liver disease). Severe symptoms warrant a 100 mL bolus of 3% saline, repeatable up to 3 times, targeting a 4–6 mEq/L rise. If you overshoot, re-lower with D5W ± desmopressin.
10. **Neurology**. Dementia with Lewy bodies: fluctuating cognition, well-formed visual hallucinations, and parkinsonism beginning within a year of the cognitive decline; REM sleep behavior disorder often precedes everything by years. Severe neuroleptic sensitivity is the tested pearl, no typical antipsychotics.
