1. **Cardiology**. Asymptomatic severe AS + LVEF <50% → AVR (Class I). Exercise testing is fine to unmask symptoms in truly asymptomatic severe AS, contraindicated once symptomatic.
2. **Psychiatry**. Serotonin syndrome: onset <24h, clonus/hyperreflexia lower-extremity predominant, mydriasis, diarrhea → cyproheptadine. NMS: days, lead-pipe rigidity, hyporeflexia, after dopamine blockade → dantrolene/bromocriptine.
3. **Allergy/Immunology**. Anaphylaxis: IM epi 0.3–0.5 mg (1 mg/mL) anterolateral thigh, repeat q5–15 min. Antihistamines/steroids treat nothing acutely and do not prevent biphasic reactions. Tryptase peaks ~1–2h; only interpretable against a baseline drawn ≥24h later.
4. **Dermatology**. SJS <10% BSA, overlap 10–30%, TEN >30%. Culprits: allopurinol, lamotrigine, TMP-SMX, aromatic anticonvulsants, nevirapine. DRESS is the look-alike: 2–8 weeks after the drug, facial edema, eosinophilia, transaminitis, steroid taper over weeks.
5. **General IM (periop)**. Functional capacity ≥4 METs → no preop stress testing regardless of risk. Continue chronic beta-blockers; never start one on the day of surgery (POISE: fewer MIs, more strokes and deaths).
6. **Rheumatology**. Urate-lowering target <6 mg/dL (<5 with tophi). Starting allopurinol during a flare is acceptable under anti-inflammatory cover. Check HLA-B*5801 before allopurinol in Han Chinese, Thai, and Korean CKD patients.
7. **Hematology**. DAT pattern separates them: IgG (±C3) = warm AIHA, steroids first. C3 only = cold agglutinin disease, keep the patient warm, steroids and splenectomy do not work, rituximab-based therapy does.
8. **Gastroenterology**. Acute pancreatitis: lactated Ringer's, moderate goal-directed fluids (WATERFALL showed aggressive resuscitation caused fluid overload with no benefit), early oral/enteral feeding, no prophylactic antibiotics even for necrosis.
9. **Infectious disease**. Neurosyphilis at any stage requires IV aqueous penicillin G 18–24 million units/day × 10–14 days; no oral or IM substitute. Desensitization is mandatory for penicillin-allergic pregnant patients.
10. **Women's health**. Postmenopausal bleeding: TVUS stripe ≤4 mm has a very high NPV and can defer biopsy, but recurrent or persistent bleeding gets endometrial sampling regardless of stripe thickness.
