Ten facts a day, written for recall rather than for reading. Specialty in bold, then the thing worth remembering.

1. **Cardiology**. HCM sudden-death risk markers that drive primary-prevention ICD: prior unexplained syncope, family history of SCD, max wall thickness ≥30 mm, NSVT on monitoring, apical aneurysm, LVEF <50%. Exertional syncope with a murmur that *increases* on Valsalva is HCM, not AS.
2. **Pulmonary**. Lung cancer screening (USPSTF 2021): annual low-dose CT, age 50–80, ≥20 pack-years, current smoker or quit within 15 years. Stop at 15 years abstinent or when a comorbidity limits curative treatment.
3. **Gastroenterology**. Celiac serology is tTG-IgA *plus a total IgA level*, selective IgA deficiency is the classic false negative (use IgG-based testing). Testing is invalid if the patient already went gluten-free; they must be eating gluten.
4. **Infectious disease**. Dexamethasone 0.15 mg/kg q6h × 4 days must be given *before or with* the first antibiotic dose in suspected bacterial meningitis. Benefit is in pneumococcal disease; giving it after antibiotics is a wrong answer.
5. **Rheumatology**. Synovial WBC >50,000 with neutrophil predominance suggests septic arthritis, but crystals do NOT exclude infection, gout and septic joint coexist. Culture and treat when the picture is septic regardless of crystal findings.
6. **Endocrinology**. SGLT2 inhibitors cause euglycemic DKA: acidosis with glucose <250, often triggered by surgery, fasting, or illness. Hold the drug 3–4 days preoperatively; a normal glucose does not rule out DKA.
7. **Hematology**. Cancer-associated VTE: DOACs are acceptable, but rivaroxaban and edoxaban raised major GI bleeding in luminal GI malignancies. Apixaban or LMWH for gastric/GI tumors; LMWH also when absorption is unreliable.
8. **Neurology**. Myasthenic crisis gets IVIG or plasma exchange; high-dose steroids alone can transiently worsen weakness. Precipitant drugs to know: fluoroquinolones, aminoglycosides, macrolides, IV magnesium, beta-blockers.
9. **Allergy/Immunology**. ~10% report penicillin allergy, <1% are truly allergic, and most labels are outgrown. Cephalosporin cross-reactivity is driven by R1 side-chain similarity, not the beta-lactam ring, it is low. Delabel; don't default to vancomycin.
10. **General IM / Prevention**. One-time abdominal ultrasound for AAA in men 65–75 who have ever smoked. Selective offering in men that age who never smoked; not recommended in women who never smoked.

Subspecialties covered: Cardiology, Pulmonary, GI, ID, Rheumatology, Endocrinology, Hematology, Neurology, Allergy/Immunology, General IM/Prevention.
