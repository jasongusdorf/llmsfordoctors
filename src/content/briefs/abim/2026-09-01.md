1. **Cardiology**. DOAC contraindications in AF: mechanical prosthetic valve and moderate-severe (rheumatic) mitral stenosis. Warfarin only. Other valve disease, bioprosthetic valves, and prior valve repair are fine for DOACs.

2. **Pulmonary**. Asthma: SABA-only rescue is no longer recommended at any severity; ICS-formoterol as reliever (± maintenance, SMART) reduces exacerbations. Rising SABA canister use is the exam's marker of poor control.

3. **Gastroenterology**. Acute pancreatitis fluids: moderate LR, not aggressive. WATERFALL stopped early for fluid overload with no benefit. LR over normal saline.

4. **Infectious disease**. Syphilis: benzathine PCN G 2.4 MU IM ×1 for primary/secondary/early latent; ×3 weekly for late latent or unknown duration; IV aqueous PCN for neuro/ocular/oto. Pregnancy = desensitize, never doxycycline. Cure = fourfold (2-dilution) nontreponemal titer drop.

5. **Rheumatology**. Gout: do NOT stop urate-lowering therapy during a flare, and it's acceptable to start allopurinol during a flare under anti-inflammatory cover. Target urate <6 mg/dL, prophylaxis 3–6 months. Check HLA-B*58:01 before allopurinol in Han Chinese, Thai, Korean CKD, and African-descent patients.

6. **Endocrinology**. SGLT2 inhibitors cause euglycemic DKA: glucose can be <250 mg/dL. Hold them ~3–4 days before surgery or during acute illness/fasting. Check ketones, not just glucose, in an acidotic patient on one.

7. **Hematology**. Warm AIHA: DAT positive for IgG, spherocytes, steroids first-line. Cold agglutinin: DAT positive for C3 only, agglutination on smear, steroids largely ineffective: keep the patient warm, rituximab if treatment needed; look for Mycoplasma, EBV, or lymphoproliferative disease.

8. **Oncology**. Lung cancer screening (USPSTF 2021): annual low-dose CT, ages 50–80, ≥20 pack-years, currently smoking or quit within 15 years. Stop once 15 years abstinent or when a comorbidity limits curative treatment.

9. **Obstetrics / Women's health**. Low-dose aspirin 81 mg from 12–28 weeks (ideally before 16) for high preeclampsia risk. Treat severe-range BP ≥160/110 acutely with labetalol, nifedipine, or hydralazine; magnesium is for eclampsia prophylaxis, not blood pressure. ACEi/ARB contraindicated.

10. **Geriatrics**. Antipsychotics do not prevent or shorten delirium; use them only for severe agitation threatening safety. Multicomponent nonpharmacologic care is the answer. Benzodiazepines worsen delirium except in alcohol/benzo withdrawal.
