Ten facts a day, written for recall rather than for reading. Specialty in bold, then the thing worth remembering.

1. **Cardiology**. AF + PCI: drop aspirin at discharge, DOAC + clopidogrel (AUGUSTUS/ENTRUST). Triple therapy only for the periprocedural window.
2. **Nephrology**. Chronic hyponatremia: max 8 mEq/L per 24h if ODS risk. Overcorrection is relowered with D5W ± desmopressin.
3. **Endocrinology**. Screen for primary aldosteronism in resistant HTN, HTN + spontaneous/diuretic-induced hypokalemia, HTN + adrenal incidentaloma, HTN + OSA, early-onset HTN or stroke <40. MRAs must be off before ARR.
4. **Infectious disease**. Asymptomatic bacteriuria: treat only in pregnancy and before urologic procedures with expected mucosal bleeding. Not for pyuria, odor, delirium alone, or catheters.
5. **Hematology**. HIT: stop all heparin, start argatroban/bivalirudin/fondaparinux. No prophylactic platelets. No warfarin until platelets >150k.
6. **Rheumatology**. GCA: steroids immediately, do not wait for biopsy; TAB stays positive ~2 weeks. Vision loss → IV methylprednisolone.
7. **Pulmonary**. Long-term O2 improves mortality in COPD only at PaO2 ≤55 (SaO2 ≤88%), or ≤59 with cor pulmonale/polycythemia. LOTT: no benefit for moderate desaturation.
8. **Gastroenterology**. SAAG ≥1.1 = portal hypertension. SBP: albumin 1.5 g/kg day 1 + 1 g/kg day 3 if Cr >1, BUN >30, or bili >4.
9. **Oncology**. Hypercalcemia of malignancy: IV saline + zoledronic acid (48-72h onset). Calcitonin for the first 24-48h only, tachyphylaxis. Denosumab if renal failure.
10. **Neurology**. NPH: gait first, then urinary, then cognition. Large-volume LP with pre/post gait testing predicts shunt response; imaging alone does not.

Subspecialties covered: Cardiology, Nephrology, Endocrinology, ID, Hematology, Rheumatology, Pulmonary, GI, Oncology, Neurology.
