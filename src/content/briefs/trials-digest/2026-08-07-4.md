**Worth reading:** the JAMA Cardiology U-shaped LV dimension analysis is the one with the broadest bedside reach. In 273,921 HF admissions, mortality rose at both ends of the LVEDD distribution, so a normal-sized ventricle in a patient with heart failure is not a reassuring finding, it is a distinct and higher-risk phenotype. The sex-specific optima (about 47 mm in men, 43 mm in women) are a useful mental anchor when you are reading an echo report on the wards.
