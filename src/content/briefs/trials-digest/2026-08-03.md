**Worth reading:** the JAMA Surgery primary hyperparathyroidism cohort is the one with the widest bedside reach. Half of 43,399 adults already had osteoporosis, fracture, stones or CKD at the moment of biochemical diagnosis, a third of the rest progressed within about 17 months, and only one in five was ever referred for parathyroidectomy. If you are the admitting hospitalist who notices the calcium, you are often the only one who will act on it. For the AI side, PRISM2 in Nature Medicine is the first slide-level pathology foundation model to match calibrated clinical-grade detection products by prompt alone.
