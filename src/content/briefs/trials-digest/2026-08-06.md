**Worth reading:** SirPAD (NEJM) is the item most likely to change practice in your lane. In an all-comers, largely pragmatic population of 1,252 patients undergoing infrainguinal endovascular treatment, sirolimus-coated balloons reduced major adverse limb events at one year from 15.0% to 8.8%, meeting both noninferiority and superiority. It is a clean answer on drug-coated balloons in PAD after years of paclitaxel-mortality litigation, and it moves the sirolimus option from plausible to evidence-backed.\
\
Runner-up: FIND-CKD extends finerenone beyond diabetic kidney disease for the first time in a dedicated trial. The eGFR slope benefit is real but modest (0.7 mL/min/1.73m2/yr), so read the hierarchical secondary endpoints before you generalize.
