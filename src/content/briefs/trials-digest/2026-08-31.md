Worth reading: PVI-SHAM-AF

The first double-blind, sham-controlled trial of AF ablation found no significant quality-of-life advantage over a sham procedure at 6 months. Both arms improved substantially, which is the point: most of the symptomatic benefit patients report after ablation may be expectation and regression to the mean rather than the lesion set.

Runner-up: the pooled ABYSS plus SMART-DECISION analysis is the cleanest evidence yet that beta-blockers can be stopped in stable post-MI patients with preserved EF.
