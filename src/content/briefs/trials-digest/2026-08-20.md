**Worth reading:** ITACS is the one with the most direct bearing on your inpatient practice. It is the largest randomized test of preoperative IV iron in anemic cardiac surgery patients, and it lands on a genuinely modest win: fewer transfusions and one extra day at home out of 90, with no signal on major complications. Whether that clears the bar for a routine preop iron pathway is a real judgment call, and the paper does not pretend otherwise.
