**Worth reading:** Finerenone in non-diabetic CKD (NEJM). This extends the nonsteroidal MRA story past diabetic kidney disease for the first time in a large trial, with a modest but real eGFR slope benefit and a composite kidney/CV hazard ratio of 0.77. The hyperkalemia signal is manageable but means potassium monitoring becomes part of the package for a much larger group of CKD patients.
