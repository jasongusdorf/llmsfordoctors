**Worth reading:** the updated global PAD estimate in Lancet Global Health. It pools 157 studies and more than 9.7 million participants to put 2023 prevalence at 6.58% of adults 25 and older, about 316.5 million people, and it is now slightly higher in women than in men. That reframes PAD as a larger and more female burden than the older 2015-era numbers most guidelines still cite. The other item to notice is the GBD 2023 morbidity gap paper: years lived in poor health grew from 8.8 to 10.7, and widened in 203 of 204 countries, which is a fairly direct rebuttal of the compression-of-morbidity hypothesis.
