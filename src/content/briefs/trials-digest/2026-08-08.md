**Worth reading:** OPTIMA-AF is the one with the most immediate ward-level consequence. In AF patients undergoing PCI, dropping dual antithrombotic therapy to a single month was non-inferior for ischemic events while halving major bleeding (4.5% vs 8.8%). That pushes further in the direction the field has been drifting for a decade, and it is the kind of number that should change what you write on discharge.
