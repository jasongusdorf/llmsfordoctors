**Worth reading:** the guanabenz vanishing white matter trial is the most interesting new item, and also the most methodologically fraught: a single-arm phase 1/2 in 33 children showing a 67% reduction in the hazard of losing ambulation against 66 matched registry controls. Real signal in a disease with nothing else, but historical controls plus hallucinations in 55% of patients means the extension study is the one that will actually settle it.

Quiet window at the top end: no NEJM original research articles appeared between August 14 and 17 (only Perspectives and Images), and the main Lancet, JAMA specialty journals, BMJ and Annals had no new major research in those days either. The August 12 to 13 drop was the substance of the week.
