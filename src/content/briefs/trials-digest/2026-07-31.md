**Worth reading:** the dapagliflozin cardiac surgery trial in JAMA. A 24 point absolute reduction in postoperative AKI from four perioperative doses of a cheap, widely available oral drug is an unusually large effect for a perioperative intervention, and it is the kind of thing that shows up on your service within a year. Worth reading the methods closely, since the AKI definition is KDIGO creatinine and urine output based and the placebo arm rate of 52% is high, which raises the question of how much of the benefit is hard kidney injury versus creatinine kinetics.
