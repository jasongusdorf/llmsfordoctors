**Worth reading:** SAVE-O2 AI. Closed-loop oxygen titration took time-in-target from 63% to 85% and cut hyperoxemia by two thirds in ordinary ward and ICU patients, with consistent performance across Monk skin tone categories. It is a process outcome, not mortality, but this is the most immediately deployable piece of inpatient automation to hit a top journal this year.
