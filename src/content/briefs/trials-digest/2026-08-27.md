**Worth reading:** the JAMA Cardiology meta-analysis of catheter-based left atrial appendage closure. Pooling all eight randomized trials against oral anticoagulation, overall stroke and major bleeding were a wash, but LAAC carried a 34% higher rate of ischemic stroke, with the bleeding advantage confined to nonprocedural events. It is the cleanest argument yet that LAAC belongs in patients who genuinely cannot take anticoagulation, not as a first-line convenience.
