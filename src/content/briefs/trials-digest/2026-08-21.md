**Worth reading:** RACER-Knee is the cleanest negative robotic-surgery trial yet. A masked, pragmatic, multicentre RCT with sham incisions and masked operative notes found the Mako system delivered no patient-perceptible benefit at 12 months and cost more. It is a useful template for how to evaluate expensive procedural technology, and a reasonable thing to cite when someone pitches a robot.
