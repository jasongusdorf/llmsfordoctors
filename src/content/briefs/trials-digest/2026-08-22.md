**Worth reading:** the mazdutide phase 2 trial in *Lancet Diabetes & Endocrinology*. An 18.1% weight reduction at 32 weeks from a glucagon/GLP-1 dual agonist puts it in tirzepatide territory, and the glucagon arm is the interesting part: it is a different mechanism for energy expenditure and hepatic fat, not just appetite. The catch is tolerability, with 20% discontinuing 16 mg for mostly GI adverse events, so the durable dose may end up lower than the headline.
