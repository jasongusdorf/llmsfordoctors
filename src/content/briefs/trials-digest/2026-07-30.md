**Worth reading:** ISLEND-1 in NEJM. A once-weekly oral pill holding viral suppression as well as daily bictegravir-emtricitabine-TAF is the first credible break from daily oral ART, and it lands the same week as the ARTISTRY-2 switch data. Second look for the hospitalist: TRACK is a clean negative that should end low-dose rivaroxaban for primary CV risk reduction in advanced CKD, with a real bleeding signal (HR 1.51).
