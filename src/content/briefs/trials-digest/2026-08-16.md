**Worth reading:** RECAP-ICH is the one with the widest bedside reach. Pooling individual data from four trials, sustained intensive BP lowering after spontaneous ICH cut recurrent stroke by nearly 40 percent, almost entirely by preventing repeat hemorrhage, with benefit appearing early and holding across baseline BP and time since the index bleed. It is the strongest case yet that long-term BP control is the cornerstone of secondary prevention after ICH.

Runner-up: the UK pragmatic TIVA trial is a clean null in 2508 older surgical patients and should quiet the propofol-versus-volatile argument for a while.
