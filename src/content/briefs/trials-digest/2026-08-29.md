**Worth reading:** AMUNDSEN is the cleanest test yet of the "hit LDL hard, immediately" instinct after MI. Starting evolocumab in the cath lab crushed LDL-C (median 16 mg/dL at 6 weeks, 82% at goal at a year) and still produced no separation in death or CV rehospitalization at 12 months. It argues against acute pleiotropic PCSK9 benefit and reframes early PCSK9 use as a durability-of-control question rather than an early-event question. CMR GUIDE cuts the same way: scar-guided ICDs in mildly reduced EF missed the composite primary endpoint despite a striking signal on sudden death alone, in a trial powered for far more events than it got.
