**Worth reading:** ICECAP is the week's biggest result for inpatient practice. In 1,158 comatose survivors of out-of-hospital cardiac arrest, extending hypothermia at 33 C beyond 6 hours bought nothing on the modified Rankin Scale, in either shockable or nonshockable rhythms, and the trial met its stopping rule at interim analysis. Combined with the 2026 LOGICAL oxygen-target trial, post-arrest care keeps converging on less intervention rather than more.

No major original trials appeared in NEJM this window; the August 6 issue's originals (LOGICAL, FIND) were published online in June.
