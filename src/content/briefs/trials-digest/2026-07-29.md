Worth reading: ADVANCE OUTCOMES (Lancet)

This is = the first event-driven phase 3 to show an oral prostacyclin-pathway agent meaningfully bending the clinical worsening curve in PAH on modern dual background therapy, halving events over a median 85 weeks. The catch is tolerability:= nearly one in five stopped ralinepag for adverse events versus 3% on placebo, so the practical question is which patients can stay on it long enough to bank the benefit.
