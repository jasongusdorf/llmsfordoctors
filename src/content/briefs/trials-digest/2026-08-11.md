**Worth reading:** the Annals target trial emulation of DOACs vs warfarin in atrial fibrillation with mitral stenosis. Guidelines still say warfarin for clinically significant mitral stenosis, and this is the best real-world data yet supporting that, with a 22% higher relative risk of ischemic stroke on DOACs at one year and no bleeding advantage. Worth knowing before the next echo report says "mild to moderate MS" and someone reaches for apixaban.
