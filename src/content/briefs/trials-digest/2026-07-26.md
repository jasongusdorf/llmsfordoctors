Worth reading

**Finerenone in CKD from glomerular disease (JAMA)** is the week's most consequential result. Glomerular disease has been largely excluded from the big finerenone cardiorenal trials, so this is the first phase 3 evidence that a nonsteroidal MRA slows eGFR decline in this population, on top of RAS blockade, with a 42% cut in albuminuria at 12 months and a hard-endpoint HR of 0.74. Read it alongside the JAMA Internal Medicine adherence cohort: we keep proving drugs work while barely half of discharged heart failure patients are still taking them at 6 months.
