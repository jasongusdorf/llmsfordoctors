**Worth reading:** ICECAP is the one that changes what you do at the bedside. A 1,158-patient adaptive trial across 71 US hospitals found the duration-response curve for post-arrest hypothermia is essentially flat, with 6 hours the shortest duration consistent with the best outcome. After TTM2 questioned whether to cool at all, this says that if you do cool, longer is not better and the current 24-hour convention has no outcome basis.
