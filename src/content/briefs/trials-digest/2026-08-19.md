**Worth reading:** the CHILL study in Lancet Public Health. It is a rare prospective natural experiment showing that a traffic policy, not a drug, changed a hard physiologic outcome: London children caught up to their comparator city on FEV1 growth within four years of the ULEZ. The effect size per year is small, but the direction and the recovery of a baseline deficit are the notable parts.
