**Worth reading:** CHIPS (JAMA) is the one with the widest downstream reach. A phase 3 adaptive trial across 27 US and Australian sites showing cold-stored platelets remain hemostatically noninferior out to 21 days would roughly triple platelet shelf life, which is a supply-chain fix more than a bedside one but changes what is available to you at 2am. Watch the signal of increased reexploration in the cold arm before treating it as settled.
