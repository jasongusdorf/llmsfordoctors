**Worth reading:** PREVAIL (JAMA Neurology). Gefurulimab is the first anti-C5 agent for generalized MG delivered as a once-weekly subcutaneous self-injection, with MG-ADL separation by week 1 and no meningococcal infections in 26 weeks. If it holds up, the practical question shifts from efficacy to whether patients can skip infusion-center visits entirely.

Note the run of well-powered negative trials this week (ARISTOTLE, PAEAN, AFRI-c, STIMULATE-ICP). Links resolve via doi.org.
