**Worth reading:** JAMA ran back-to-back incretin trials on August 4. GLORY-2 puts mazdutide 9 mg at roughly 16.7% weight loss at 60 weeks, in the tirzepatide range but in a Chinese population where obesity pharmacotherapy data have been thin. ACHIEVE-5 is the more immediately practical one for the wards: oral orforglipron added to titrated basal insulin dropped HbA1c about a full point beyond placebo, which is the first solid look at a small-molecule oral GLP-1 layered onto insulin rather than replacing it.
