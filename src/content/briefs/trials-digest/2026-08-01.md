**Worth reading:** ADVANCE OUTCOMES is the standout of the week. A 55% reduction in clinical worsening with an oral prostacyclin-pathway agent is the kind of effect size PAH has waited a long time for, and it lands in patients already on dual background therapy. Expect it to reshape the escalation algorithm before parenteral prostacyclin.
