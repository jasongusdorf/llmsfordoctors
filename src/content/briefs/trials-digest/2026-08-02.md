**Worth reading:** OPTIMA-AF is the one with the most direct floor impact. Halving bleeding by stopping antiplatelet at one month post-PCI in AF, without an ischemic penalty signal, pushes the shortest-duration end of the DAT debate further than prior trials. Runner-up for inpatient practice is ASTARTE, which gives a genuine carbapenem-sparing option for 3GC-resistant Enterobacterales bacteremia.
