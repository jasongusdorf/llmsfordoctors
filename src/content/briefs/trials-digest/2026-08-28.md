Worth reading

CARDIO-TTRansform is the story of the week. The largest ATTR-CM trial ever run, 1,432 patients, and eplontersen still failed its primary composite despite deep TTR knockdown. The likely lesson is not that silencing does not work but that the comparator has moved: most of this population was already on a stabilizer, and the event rate a contemporary trial has to beat is much lower than it was in ATTR-ACT. Read it next to HELIOS-B, where vutrisiran did hit, and the difference in background therapy is the thing to argue about.

Two NEJM rows are flagged as unverified: both published this morning with the ESC Hot Line and the full text was still paywalled when this went out. Everything else has been checked against the source abstract.
