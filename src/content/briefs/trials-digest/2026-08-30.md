**Worth reading:** PRESC1SE-MI is the one with the most direct bearing on how your ED colleagues run chest pain. It is the first large pragmatic cluster-randomized test of actually implementing the guideline-endorsed 0/1 h high-sensitivity troponin pathway, and the headline is split: safety held up at 30 days, but the promised throughput gain never materialized. The lesson is that the rule-out algorithm is not the bottleneck; everything downstream of it is.
