**Worth reading:** Finerenone in CKD without diabetes (NEJM, Aug 6). This is the first large trial to carry mineralocorticoid receptor antagonism cleanly outside diabetic kidney disease, and it hits both the eGFR slope and the composite kidney/CV endpoint. The effect is modest and hyperkalemia is real (17.0% vs 13.3%), so the practical question on your service will be who gets it on top of an SGLT2 inhibitor and a RAS blocker, and how hard you are willing to chase potassium to keep them on it.
