**Worth reading:** FIND-CKD (NEJM, Aug 6). Finerenone slowed eGFR decline in chronic kidney disease *without* diabetes, the population where the MRA evidence base has been thinnest. The absolute effect is modest (0.7 mL/min/1.73m2 per year) but the composite kidney/CV hazard ratio of 0.77 is the kind of signal that moves nephrology practice, and it will start showing up on your consult recommendations.
