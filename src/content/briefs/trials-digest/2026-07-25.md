**Worth reading:** KEYNOTE-B15/EV-304 in NEJM is the big one. Perioperative enfortumab vedotin plus pembrolizumab beat neoadjuvant cisplatin-gemcitabine on event-free survival, overall survival and pathologic complete response in *cisplatin-eligible* muscle-invasive bladder cancer, which is the population cisplatin has owned for decades. The accompanying editorial asks whether this is the end of the cisplatin era; grade 3+ toxicity was higher, so the tradeoff is real.
